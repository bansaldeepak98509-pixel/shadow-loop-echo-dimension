Shadow Loop – Privacy Policy

App: Shadow Loop: Echo Dimension
Android package: com.shadowloop.echodimension

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Shadow Loop: Echo Dimension (“the App”) handles information when you play it. The App is a puzzle game. It does not require you to create an account or provide your name, phone number, or email address to play.

1. Information stored on your device

The App stores game-related information locally on your device using the device's app storage, including, where applicable:

  • level progress and completed levels;
  • best scores or step counts;
  • game preferences, such as sound and visual-effects settings; and
  • tutorial or hint-related game state.

This information is used to operate the game and remember your progress. The App's game code does not send this locally stored game progress to a game account or a developer-operated game server. You can remove locally stored App data through your Android device settings, or by uninstalling the App. Removing data or uninstalling the App may erase your progress.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob to provide optional rewarded advertisements. An advertisement is requested when you choose an action such as “Watch Ad” to unlock an in-game hint. You can play the game without watching an advertisement.

When the advertising SDK is used, Google and its advertising partners may collect or receive information from the device and about interactions with the App. Depending on your device, settings, region, and consent choices, this may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • advertising identifiers or other device/app identifiers;
  • ad views, taps, and interactions with advertising;
  • information about app launches and interactions relevant to ad delivery; and
  • diagnostic, performance, and fraud-prevention information.

Google processes information under its own policies and terms. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Consent and privacy choices

Where required by applicable law and Google's consent requirements, a consent message provided through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) may be shown so that you can make or change relevant advertising privacy choices. The availability and wording of choices can depend on your location and applicable requirements. You can also use Android's available device settings to manage advertising identifiers and related preferences.

4. How information is used and shared

Locally stored game information is used to provide game features and preserve progress on your device. Advertising-related information is processed by Google and, where applicable, its advertising partners to provide and measure ads, prevent fraud or abuse, and support the advertising service. The developer does not operate a separate game account service or game-progress server in the current version described by this policy.

5. Data security and retention

Game progress remains in the App's local storage until it is cleared or the App is uninstalled. Information processed by Google through its advertising services is subject to Google's security, retention, and deletion practices and policies. Data transmitted by Google's services is handled according to Google's technical and privacy documentation.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. The App does not ask players to create accounts or submit personal details. Advertising services may have their own age-related requirements and settings. If you believe information has been provided to the developer in connection with the App, contact us using the email below.

7. Your choices

  • You may choose not to watch optional rewarded advertisements.
  • You may clear the App's stored data or uninstall the App to remove locally stored game progress.
  • You may review Google's privacy information and use applicable consent or device settings to manage advertising choices.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App's features, advertising setup, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this page, with its updated date shown near the top.
